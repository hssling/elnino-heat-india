This covers:

- `pipeline/reference/` (unit frame, source list, reviewed exceptions and QC reviews);

Attribution: cite the paper (see README.md) or this repository (CITATION.cff), and the original sources below.

The farmer-suicide data are aggregated state-year counts. Please follow the responsible-reporting note in README.md when using or reporting them.
